## The brief
A fictional chamber ensemble needs a reliable link to send to presenters and concert organizers. Its press kit should make routine requests easier: the right biography length, a usable photograph, a representative recording, and a booking contact.

## Put the essentials on one page
- A concise ensemble introduction, with short and full biography downloads.
- Two selected recordings, labeled with composer, work, performers, and recording context.
- A web photograph and a print-resolution download, each with the approved credit.
- A repertoire or program document, dated so presenters can tell whether it is current.
- A clearly labeled booking contact.

## A sample asset manifest
Use descriptive filenames such as `ensemble-short-bio.txt`, `ensemble-full-bio.pdf`, and `ensemble-press-photo-print.jpg`. Keep the page labels human-readable, include file types and sizes, and place the photo credit beside the download.

These are proposed filenames, not available downloads. Actual press assets would be supplied and approved by the ensemble before publication.

## Editorial checks
Confirm names, ensemble membership, work titles, credits, and contact information with one designated approver. Keep the web biography and downloads in sync. Check permission to publish each photograph and recording.

## Keep sharing simple
The press page should work as a stable link without an account. Provide biography text on the page as well as downloads so organizers can copy approved text without opening a PDF.

## How I would review it
Ask an organizer to locate a short biography and print photograph. Test keyboard access and download behavior, then check the page whenever membership, programs, or contact details change.
